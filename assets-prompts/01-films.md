Two 8-second clips, stitched to 10.4 seconds. It plays ONCE with the page
held still, the slogan arriving line by line on top of it:

| Time | Line on screen |
|---|---|
| 0.5s | قهوة زاكية. |
| 3.3s | كل يوم. |
| 6.1s | بسعر منطقي. |
| end | the frame freezes, the page opens |

So: i1 carries the first two lines (calm, one subject, room for text on
the RIGHT third of the frame), i2 carries the last line and must END on a
frame that can hold still as a photograph.
